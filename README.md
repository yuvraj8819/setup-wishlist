# Setup & Wishlist

My current tech setup and wishlist, with Indian prices (MRP, new) and running totals.

Open `index.html` in a browser. Tick wishlist items as you buy them, and tap any price to change it. Ticks and price edits are saved in your browser.

## Install on your phone

Open the site in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android), then choose **Add to Home Screen**. It opens full screen with its own gaming PC icon, and works offline after the first visit.
